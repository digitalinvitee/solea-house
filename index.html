The Philosophy

A different kind of escape

Hidden among the Mediterranean landscape of Mallorca, SOLÉA HOUSE is a place designed for those who appreciate the quieter things in life.

Natural materials, thoughtful architecture and moments of stillness come together to create an experience that feels both intimate and unforgettable.

The Hotel

Where architecture meets nature

A restored stone estate and a handful of low garden pavilions, set between olive terraces. Every wall is local limestone or lime plaster. Every room opens to the air.

Built from the island’s own stone. Kept deliberately small.

Location

A place to find your own pace

SOLÉA HOUSE is imagined in the quiet south-east of Mallorca, in the low hills behind Santanyí. Coves to the east, salt flats to the south, and stone villages that still keep a weekly market.
